# Provanta — Provable Finance Intelligence

> A public product showcase for governed, evidence-backed finance investigation and decision support.

[**Open the live demo**](https://provanta-finance-intelligence.vercel.app/)

Provanta is an AI-native finance intelligence concept for CFOs, FP&A leaders, Finance/AI teams and operators who need more than dashboards or ungrounded commentary. It connects operational signals to governed financial results, investigates material variances, evaluates explicit scenarios and presents evidence suitable for human review.

## The decision journey

**Signal → Investigation → Scenario → AI Explanation → Proof → Decision**

1. **Signal** — Surface material performance, forecast and working-capital movements.
2. **Investigation** — Trace a governed metric into ranked, reconciled drivers.
3. **Scenario** — Apply explicit assumptions and compare deterministic outcomes.
4. **AI Explanation** — Explain governed results without treating the model as the financial calculator.
5. **Proof** — Preserve definitions, lineage, reconciliation state and supporting evidence.
6. **Decision** — Give finance leaders a clear basis for review and action.

## Key capabilities

- CFO-oriented performance command center
- Variance and driver investigation
- Scenario and driver exploration
- Governed AI finance explanation
- Forecast, profitability and working-capital intelligence
- Risk and opportunity framing
- Evidence-backed proof and replay concepts
- Explicit handling of unsupported or insufficient-evidence questions

## Governed finance architecture

Provanta separates responsibilities at a high level:

```text
Governed data and semantics
        ↓
Deterministic finance calculation
        ↓
Investigation and scenario services
        ↓
Controlled AI explanation
        ↓
Evidence, proof and decision support
```

Financial arithmetic, metric meaning and reconciliation remain governed. AI is used to orchestrate approved capabilities and communicate validated results; it is not the authority for company-specific financial truth.

## Validated demonstration scope

The current demonstration includes working product journeys for executive performance, variance investigation and scenario analysis, supported by synthetic finance and operating data. Capabilities are presented with explicit evidence, reconciliation and limitation states.

This repository is intentionally a **showcase only**. It does not contain Provanta source code, proprietary formulas, prompts, datasets, generators, schemas, tests, workflows or infrastructure configuration.

## Synthetic data

All organizations, transactions, employees, customers, suppliers, metrics, scenarios and evidence shown in the demonstration are synthetic. They do not represent a real company or confidential customer information.

## Limitations

- Demonstration and research prototype; not a production SaaS service
- No claim of live SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Dynamics, Fabric, Snowflake or other vendor integration
- No claim of SOC 2, ISO 27001 or other certification
- Outputs require qualified human review
- Not financial, investment, accounting, audit, tax or legal advice
- Some product areas are intentionally shown as future direction or constrained by available evidence

## Future direction — Decision Memory

Provanta's longer-term direction is **Decision Memory**: a governed record of what was known, what assumptions were made, what evidence supported a decision and how the outcome later compared with expectations. The goal is decision continuity and accountable learning—not autonomous approval or replacement of finance leadership.
